04 — How it works

Three steps, one evidence sheet.

Inkprint sits quietly behind the writing tools teachers and students already use.

01

Capture the process

A lightweight editor, browser extension, and Docs/Word add-in record keystrokes, pauses, revisions, and paste events while the student writes.

02

Compare to baseline

Every submission is measured against that student's own verified prior work — not a global AI-vs-human model. The baseline strengthens with use.

03

Read the evidence

Teachers get a one-page evidence document, not a percentage. Flagged passages link to the process moments that produced them.

05 — Product · Evidence Panel

The microscope, not the verdict.

Essay · A. Marquez · Grade 11 1,284 words · 92 min · 14 revisions

The novel opens with an image of confinement that recurs through Chapter Three — the rusted gate, the locked study, the curtain drawn against the harbor. Marlowe's prose accumulates these symbols slowly, almost patiently, so that by the time the inheritance is announced the reader already feels the shape of the house closing in.

Furthermore, the symbolism of confinement serves as a profound metaphor that effectively underscores the protagonist's internal struggle, thereby enriching the narrative's thematic depth. In contrast, the second chapter eases the grip slightly: there is the morning at the dock, the open sail, the unforced laughter of strangers.

The reader notices, though, that even these scenes are framed by windows — and the windows are always shut.

06 — Brand · Color

Parchment, ink, and a careful drop of coral.

The page should feel like paper — never punitive. Coral is a highlighter, not a wall paint.

Ink Blue#1B2A4E · Primary
Parchment#F6F1E7 · Page
Signal Coral#E26D5A · Accent
Paper White#FFFFFF · Surfaces
Slate Gray#6B7280 · Body text
Coral 200#F7C9C0 · Flag bg
Moss#3F6B4E · Inconclusive
Sand#EADFC6 · Dividers

Rules. No surface should be more than ~8% coral. Never use red — red is punishment, and Inkprint is not punishment. Parchment, not pure white; pure white only for cards floating above the page.
