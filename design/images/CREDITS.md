## Brand alignment

All six match the *Literary & Academic* visual direction in `BRANDING.md` §7.1:
- Annotated essays, handwritten margin notes, close-ups of printed text
- Warm white-balance, natural light, no stock-photo gloss
- No robots, no glowing brains, no neural-net imagery, no blue-tinted "tech" photography

## How to use

- **Hero:** `01-fountain-pen-on-paper.jpg` cropped wide, parchment overlay at 20–30%, headline on left.
- **How it works step illustrations:** `02-open-book-handwritten-notes.jpg` and `04-book-margin-notes.jpg` — these literally show what Inkprint analyzes.
- **Research / methodology page:** `03-dictionary-close-up.jpg`.
- **For institutions:** `05-open-book-on-table.jpg` — quieter, more procurement-friendly.
- **Blog headers:** `06-open-book-text.jpg`, rotate with the others.
